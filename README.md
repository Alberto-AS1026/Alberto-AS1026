## 🛠️ From the workbench to the rack

I like understanding **how things work on the inside**: from opening up a laptop to replace a component to configuring VLANs on a Cisco switch. At **NU10 Repairs (Cork, Ireland)** I repaired devices for individual and business customers, optimized Windows and macOS to extend their lifespan, and explained every diagnosis in English to non-technical people.

At **Oficopyer (Chiclana)** I provided first-line user support, maintained office equipment and printing systems, and documented every incident in the ticketing system so nothing got lost between shifts.

Outside of work I build and test my own environments: virtual machines on **Proxmox** and **VirtualBox**, containerized services with **Docker**, **Raspberry Pi** projects and traffic analysis with **Wireshark**. I have worked with several versions of **Windows Server**, **Ubuntu Server**, **Fedora** and **Kali Linux**.

What motivates me most is **closing an incident from start to finish**: finding the fault, fixing it and making sure it doesn't come back.
